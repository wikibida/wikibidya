Abel Dagnew (born November 25, 2009), known online as @redp1s, is an Ethiopian student, web developer, streetwear designer, and retail financial trader based in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia.[1] He is recognized for building lightweight, dark-mode minimalist web applicationsmost notably the Grade 12 examination platform MatricPrepas well as founding the independent streetwear concept Deovictori and digital trading communities Wolf Wallet Street and Black Funded.[2]

Balancing secondary school studies in the Social Science stream with independent software engineering and financial market analysis, Dagnew represents a generation of young digital builders in East Africa utilizing single-file web architectures, decentralized payment rails, and minimalist brand aesthetics.[3]

Early life and education

[]

Abel Dagnew was born on November 25, 2009, in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, into a wealthy and prominent family with extensive holdings in commercial real estate.[1] His family owns major commercial buildings and landmark properties throughout Ethiopia as well as international real estate assets across multiple countries.[1] Raised in the Ethiopian capital within an entrepreneurial environment, Abel Dagnew developed an early interest in digital design, online commerce, and self-directed technology education alongside his formal schooling.

Abel Dagnew attends Green School in Addis Ababa, pursuing the Social Science stream as part of the Class of 2027.[4] His academic curriculum focuses on economics, geography, history, and mathematics in preparation for the Ethiopian Secondary School Leaving Certificate Examination (ESSLCE / EEUE) in 2027.

Beyond his domestic secondary studies, Abel Dagnew has structured an international higher education pathway targeting France for the 2028 academic intake. His academic roadmap includes intensive French language acquisition through a Diplôme Universitaire de Français Langue Étrangère (DU-FLE) program, followed by undergraduate studies toward a Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) in Paris.[4]

Technology and web development

[]

As a self-taught web developer, Abel Dagnew specializes in high-speed, zero-bloat web applications built primarily with single-file HTML5, modern CSS, vanilla JavaScript, and Supabase cloud backends, deployed via distributed static cloud hosting and custom web servers.[2] His engineering philosophy favors dark-mode minimalism, instant client-side responsiveness, and accessible mobile-first interfaces.

MatricPrep (2026)

In 2026, Abel Dagnew developed MatricPrep, an interactive, AI-assisted Grade 12 examination preparation platform tailored for Ethiopian students preparing for national entrance exams.[2] The platform combines curated practice assessments, subject-specific study modules for both Natural and Social Science streams, and automated study guidance within a distraction-free dark interface.

Web3 & Crypto Payment Interfaces

Abel Dagnew has also engineered custom cryptocurrency checkout interfaces designed for digital storefronts and community memberships. These lightweight payment modules integrate wallet verification and transaction tracking across the Polygon and BEP-20 (BNB Smart Chain) networks, enabling low-fee global digital transactions.[5]

Fashion, design, and aesthetics

[]

Abel Dagnew is the founder and creative director of Deovictori, an independent streetwear concept defined by dark-mode minimalism, monochrome palettes, metallic and chrome typography, and symmetrical leetspeak branding exemplified by his handle @redp1s.[7]

His visual direction merges high-contrast gothic and industrial motifs with contemporary digital minimalism. Creative influences cited in his design language include avant-garde luxury and underground streetwear aesthetics (such as Chrome Hearts and Esdeekid) alongside modern hip-hop culture, notably artists like Playboi Carti and NBA YoungBoy.[7]
